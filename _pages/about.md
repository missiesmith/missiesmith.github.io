I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at Auburn University and Director of the Cognitive Engineering and Computing (CEC) Lab. My research focuses on human-systems integration and human–technology interaction, with the goal of designing safer, more effective, and more intuitive systems. My research integrates human factors, cognitive engineering, and emerging technologies to understand how people interact with complex systems. Current research areas include immersive technologies, augmented reality interfaces, driver interaction and distraction, human–AI interaction, and adaptive information presentation.
